**Why Anthropic’s Birth Matters**

According to *Wired*’s analysis, Anthropic’s early work on “Constitutional AI”—a framework that embeds a set of high‑level principles directly into language models—provided a concrete, testable approach that contrasted with the more abstract safety conjectures dominating the field at the time. The article cites internal memos from Anthropic’s first year, showing how the team prioritized interpretability and robustness, laying groundwork that later influenced both industry and academic circles.
