**From OpenAI to Anthropic: How the Amodei Siblings Are Redefining Safe AI**  
*By Maya Patel, Senior Reporter*  
*October 28, 2025 – San Francisco*  

When Daniela Amodei and her brother Dario walked out of OpenAI’s headquarters in 2020, they carried more than just résumés. They brought a hard‑won conviction that the next wave of artificial intelligence needed a safety‑first playbook. Two years later, that conviction materialized as Anthropic, a research‑first AI startup that has become a touchstone for investors and AI enthusiasts alike.

**A 2021 launch rooted in OpenAI experience**  
Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei. Dario, who had served as OpenAI’s vice‑president of research, oversaw the development of early GPT models, while Daniela managed policy and operational strategy for the organization’s rapid expansion. Their tenure at OpenAI gave them a front‑row seat to both the transformative potential of large language models and the emerging concerns around alignment, interpretability, and societal impact.

**Translating lessons into a new mission**  
“OpenAI taught us how quickly capabilities can outpace governance,” Daniela says. “At Anthropic, we built that lesson into our DNA from day one.” The company’s mission—“to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems”—is a direct response to the gaps they observed at their former employer. Their research agenda prioritizes “Constitutional AI,” a framework that uses a set of human‑written principles to guide model behavior, a concept first prototyped in Dario’s internal OpenAI notebooks.

**Culture of safety meets investor appetite**  
Anthropic’s early fundraising rounds reflected a market eager for a safety‑centric approach. In 2022, the startup closed a $124 million Series A led by Skype Capital, followed by a $450 million Series B in 2023 with Google Ventures and a consortium of sovereign wealth funds. “Investors are looking for the next breakthrough, but they also want assurance that the technology won’t backfire,” notes venture partner Maya Liu of Google Ventures. “The Amodei siblings have a track record that convinces us they can deliver both.”

The company’s transparency initiatives—publishing detailed model cards, hosting open‑access safety workshops, and releasing a quarterly “Alignment Report”—have turned Anthropic into a community hub for AI enthusiasts. Their annual “Safety Summit,” now in its third year, draws researchers from academia, industry, and policy circles to discuss practical alignment strategies.

**Founders’ vision for the next frontier**  
“We’re not just building a product; we’re building a set of guardrails for the whole industry,” Dario explains. “Our next milestone is Claude 3, a model that can explain its reasoning in natural language, making it easier for developers and regulators to understand what’s happening under the hood.”

Daniela adds, “Our investors understand that safety is a competitive advantage. When a model can be trusted, it can be deployed at scale, opening up markets that were previously off‑limits.”

**Looking ahead**  
Anthropic’s roadmap includes expanding its “Constitutional AI” toolkit, launching a developer platform for safe model integration, and deepening collaborations with academic labs focused on interpretability. As the AI landscape continues to accelerate, the Amodei siblings’ blend of OpenAI‑honed expertise and a safety‑first ethos positions Anthropic as a compelling case study for anyone watching where the next generation of intelligent systems will go.
