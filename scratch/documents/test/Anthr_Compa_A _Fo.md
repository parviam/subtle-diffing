## Founders’ Corner  
*By Daniela Amodei, Chief Executive Officer*

When Dario and I left OpenAI in early 2021, we carried with us a deep respect for the breakthroughs we had helped achieve—and a lingering concern about the long‑term implications of rapidly scaling language models. It was that mix of excitement and responsibility that led us to found Anthropic later that year. **Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei**, a fact that still feels like the opening line of a story we are living together.

### Lessons from OpenAI

At OpenAI, we witnessed firsthand how powerful generative models could become almost overnight. The culture there emphasized bold experimentation, but we also saw the growing pains: alignment gaps, unexpected behaviors, and the difficulty of communicating model limitations to a broad audience. Those experiences taught us three things that have become the pillars of Anthropic’s mission:

1. **Safety First, Not an Afterthought** – We learned that safety must be baked into the research loop, not tacked on later. This insight drove us to create a dedicated Safety Research team from day one, whose work now informs every iteration of our Claude series.

2. **Interpretability as a Core Capability** – Understanding why a model makes a particular prediction is essential for trust. Our early work on “steerability” and “transparent reasoning” stems directly from the opaque black‑box challenges we faced at OpenAI.

3. **Scalable Governance** – As models grow, so does the need for robust governance frameworks. The governance structures we experimented with at OpenAI inspired Anthropic’s own “Responsible AI Charter,” which guides product releases, partnership decisions, and internal research priorities.

### Shaping Anthropic’s Mission

Those lessons have crystallized into a mission that feels both familiar and distinct: **to build AI systems that are reliable, interpretable, and aligned with human intent**. Since our launch, we have:

- Released **Claude 3.0** (2022) and **Claude 3.5 “Insight”** (2024), each iteration improving safety metrics by double‑digit percentages while expanding capabilities.
- Published the **Anthropic Safety Report** (2023) and the **Interpretability Playbook** (2024), open‑sourcing tools that the broader community can adopt.
- Partnered with academic institutions to fund research on “value learning” and “robustness under distribution shift,” topics that were merely speculative when we left OpenAI.

Every product decision, every research paper, and every partnership is filtered through the lens of those early OpenAI experiences. It’s a constant reminder that the power of AI is matched only by the responsibility we bear.

### Looking Ahead

The next year will be about scaling responsibly. We are piloting **Claude 4**, which will incorporate a new “self‑verification” layer—an idea born from the alignment challenges we observed in 2021. We also plan to expand our **Safety Fellowship** program, inviting external scholars to work alongside our team on the toughest open problems.

I am grateful to the entire Anthropic family—engineers, researchers, and staff—who have turned those early lessons into tangible progress. As we continue this journey, I remain convinced that the best way to honor our origins is to keep pushing the boundaries of what safe, trustworthy AI can achieve.

Thank you for being part of this story.

*With optimism,*  
**Daniela Amodei**  
Chief Executive Officer, Anthropic
