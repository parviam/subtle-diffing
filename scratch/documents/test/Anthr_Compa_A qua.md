## Our History – A Timeline for New Hires  

| Year | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| **2021** | **Founding of Anthropic** – Former OpenAI researchers **Daniela Amodei** and **Dario Amodei** launched Anthropic with the vision of creating AI systems that are safe and aligned from the ground up. |
| **2022** | Release of **Claude‑1**, our first internal language model, accompanied by a peer‑reviewed paper on “Constitutional AI” that set a new standard for safety‑first development. |
| **2023** | **Series B Funding** – Secured $450 M led by a coalition of venture partners, enabling rapid expansion of research teams and the opening of a new office in Seattle. |
| **2024 (Q1)** | Launch of **Claude‑2**, featuring improved reasoning capabilities and a 30 % reduction in hallucination rates across benchmark tests. |
| **2024 (Q3)** | Introduction of **Claude‑3**, our most advanced model to date, now integrated with major cloud providers for enterprise‑grade deployment. |
| **2024 (Q3)** | **Employee Milestone** – Anthropic surpasses 600 full‑time staff worldwide, reflecting our commitment to diverse talent and interdisciplinary collaboration. |
| **2024 (Q4 – upcoming)** | Planned rollout of **Anthropic Safety Lab**, a dedicated research hub focused on interpretability and robustness in large‑scale AI systems. |

*Tip for new hires:* Keep this timeline handy as a quick reference during onboarding sessions and team introductions. It’s a great conversation starter!
