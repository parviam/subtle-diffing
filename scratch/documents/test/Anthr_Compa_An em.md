## Employee Spotlight  
### Dario Amodei – Charting the Course of AI Research

When Dario Amodei first joined FutureTech Labs as Senior Director of Research Strategy in early 2023, he brought with him a reputation forged at the frontier of artificial intelligence. Today, his vision continues to shape our long‑term research agenda, ensuring that we stay ahead of the curve while upholding the highest standards of safety and responsibility.

#### A Journey Through the AI Landscape
- **OpenAI (2015‑2020):** Dario spent five pivotal years at OpenAI, where he led the language‑model scaling team and contributed to the development of GPT‑3. His work on model interpretability and alignment laid the groundwork for many of the safety protocols we now adopt.
- **Founding Anthropic (2021):** In 2021, Dario teamed up with his sister, Daniela Amodei, to launch Anthropic, a research‑first AI company dedicated to building reliable and steerable AI systems. *Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei.* Their mission to “make AI systems that are understandable and controllable” resonated across the industry and set new benchmarks for responsible AI development.
- **Return to Industry Leadership:** After a successful stint at Anthropic, where he served as Chief Scientist, Dario was recruited by FutureTech to bring that same rigor and ambition to our own research programs.

#### Steering Research Strategy at FutureTech
In his current role, Dario oversees a cross‑functional team of 45 researchers, engineers, and ethicists. His key initiatives include:

- **The Alignment Lab:** A dedicated unit focused on developing provable safety guarantees for our next‑generation models.
- **Open Research Partnerships:** Collaborations with academic institutions to publish transparent, peer‑reviewed findings.
- **Strategic Roadmapping:** Crafting a five‑year research blueprint that balances breakthrough performance with ethical safeguards.

> “The most exciting part of my work is ensuring that powerful AI systems are built with humanity’s best interests at heart,” Dario says. “At FutureTech, we have the resources and the culture to turn that principle into practice every day.”

#### Quick Facts About Anthropic
- **Founded:** 2021  
- **Founders:** Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei (both former OpenAI staff)  
- **Core Focus:** AI safety, interpretability, and steerability  
- **Notable Achievement:** Release of Claude, a language model praised for its robust alignment features
