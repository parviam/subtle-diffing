**Anthropic’s IPO Looms: Analyst Predicts $15 Billion Valuation**  
*By Kevin Liu, Senior Technology Analyst*  
*Wall Street Journal – New York, Oct. 28 2025*

Anthropic, the AI‑research startup best known for its Claude series of large‑language models, is poised to go public later this year, and the market may be looking at a valuation north of $15 billion, according to my latest analysis. The company’s trajectory—marked by rapid revenue growth, a deep‑pocketed investor base, and a distinctive focus on AI safety—places it among the most compelling tech IPOs on the 2025 calendar.

**Founding pedigree fuels investor confidence**  
Anthropic was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI employees Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei. Their experience at OpenAI, where they helped shape early large‑model research, gave Anthropic an instant credibility boost. The Amodei siblings leveraged that background to attract marquee backers such as Google Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz, and a strategic investment from Amazon Web Services. The “ex‑OpenAI” tag continues to be a key valuation driver, as investors view the founders’ insider knowledge of large‑scale model development and safety protocols as a competitive moat.

**Product differentiation and market traction**  
Since its debut, Anthropic’s Claude models have been positioned as “safer” alternatives to rival offerings, emphasizing reduced hallucination rates and more transparent alignment techniques. Enterprise customers—including several Fortune‑500 firms in finance, healthcare, and retail—have signed multi‑year contracts that now generate an estimated $450 million in annual recurring revenue (ARR). The company’s subscription‑based pricing, combined with a growing ecosystem of third‑party plugins, suggests ARR could surpass $1 billion by the end of 2026.

**Funding history and balance sheet strength**  
Anthropic has raised roughly $4.5 billion across four funding rounds, most recently a $2 billion Series E in March 2024 that valued the private company at $13 billion. The cash runway extends well into 2027, giving the firm flexibility to invest in next‑generation model research and expand its data‑center footprint without immediate pressure to monetize every new release.

**Valuation comparables**  
When benchmarked against peers—OpenAI (valued at $27 billion in its last private round), Google DeepMind, and Microsoft‑backed AI ventures—Anthropic’s projected $15 billion IPO valuation reflects a premium for its safety‑first positioning. The market is currently rewarding AI firms that can demonstrate robust governance frameworks, a trend that aligns with heightened regulatory scrutiny in the U.S. and EU.

**Risks to consider**  
Regulatory developments remain the primary headwind. The European Union’s AI Act, slated for full implementation in 2026, could impose compliance costs that affect profit margins. Additionally, the rapid pace of model scaling by larger cloud providers could erode Anthropic’s differentiation unless it continues to innovate on alignment techniques.

**IPO timeline and price range**  
Based on the company’s current financials and comparable IPOs, I anticipate Anthropic filing an S‑1 by early 2026, with a likely listing on the NYSE in the second half of the year. Assuming a price‑to‑sales multiple of 12‑15×—in line with recent AI‑focused listings—the IPO could price shares between $28 and $35, translating to a market cap of roughly $14.5‑$16 billion.

**Conclusion**  
Anthropic’s blend of seasoned leadership, a clear safety narrative, and solid revenue growth positions it as a standout candidate for a high‑profile public debut. Investors who value both cutting‑edge AI capability and responsible development may find the upcoming offering particularly attractive.
